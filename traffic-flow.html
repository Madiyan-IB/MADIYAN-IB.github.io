DESIGN, WORKING DEMO & TESTING

Project overview

Traffic monitoring requires more than finding objects in a frame: it must identify the same vehicle across frames and count movements without duplication. This browser lab illustrates the association and line-crossing concepts on synthetic traffic observations.

Implementation details

  • Model a CCTV scene with cars moving in opposite directions and passing through a shared counting line.
  • Compare nearest-center associations with a motion/appearance-aware toy tracker during crossings and partial occlusions.
  • Show assigned track IDs, trajectories, synthetic directional counts, and cumulative ID changes.
  • Use frame scrubbing, playback and scenario controls to inspect counting events.

Testing & evaluation

  • Check how identity consistency changes near vehicle crossings and missing observations.
  • Verify simulated inbound and outbound crossings against known scripted vehicle paths.
  • An operational system would require real annotated traffic videos, counting error, IDF1/HOTA and throughput benchmarks; none are claimed here.

Architecture

Synthetic CCTV frames→Vehicle observations→Track association→Virtual line counts→Traffic-flow indicators

Demo boundaries

This page runs original JavaScript toy tracking over synthetic traffic observations, not a real CCTV feed or YOLO/ByteTrack inference. Directional crossing counts are derived from scripted positions; no operational traffic-monitoring results are claimed.
