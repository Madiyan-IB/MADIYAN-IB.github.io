Behind the build.

PROJECT NOTES / 02

Project overview

A computer vision pipeline designed to follow specific vehicles in crowded traffic footage. It uses YOLO detections to locate plate regions, OpenCV preprocessing to prepare image crops, and Tesseract OCR for character recognition.

My contributions

  • Applied object detection to isolate vehicles and plate regions in video frames.
  • Used grayscale conversion and thresholding to prepare license plate crops for OCR.
  • Extracted characters using Tesseract OCR and connected observations through a tracking workflow.
  • Worked across bounding-box annotation, image preparation, model training, and performance evaluation.

Engineering approach

Detection, recognition, and tracking are separate parts of the pipeline. The project brought those stages together to make it easier to follow a target vehicle rather than treating every video frame independently.

How the pieces connect

Video frames→YOLO detection→OpenCV preprocessing→Tesseract OCR→Track association
