PROJECT NOTES / 04

Project overview

A multi-frame computer vision project combining bounding-box detections with spatial and feature matching to follow objects across video sequences and maintain a consistent target identity.

My contributions

  • Connected YOLO detections with OpenCV frame-by-frame processing.
  • Used spatial and feature matching to associate a target across consecutive frames.
  • Prepared labeled image/video data for detection and verification exercises.
  • Worked across image preprocessing, bounding-box annotation, training, and evaluation.

Engineering approach

Per-frame detection does not solve identity continuity by itself. The challenge is associating detections as objects move, cross paths, or temporarily disappear from view.

How the pieces connect

Video frame→Object detection→Spatial / feature match→Track ID update→Next frame
